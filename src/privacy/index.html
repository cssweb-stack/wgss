Privacy Policy

Privacy information for this demonstration.

This page describes the features configured in the WGSS demonstration website. It should be reviewed before an official department launch.

{% include "components/space-medium.njk" %}

This website contains demonstration content for reviewing shared layouts and navigation. Its placeholder pages do not establish academic requirements, deadlines, events, or department policies.

No site analytics, live social feed, or information submission form is configured in this demonstration. The site search is a separate form that sends a query to Google when submitted.

The search is restricted to the WGSS demonstration’s published URL. Search results depend on whether Google has indexed those pages.

The site loads Bootstrap styles and scripts from jsDelivr and the Inter font from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to those providers when requesting their resources.

Email links open your email application. If you choose to send a message, the recipient receives your email address and the information you include.

For website questions, contact the department webmaster at cssweb@hawaii.edu. The University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa Privacy Statement is available at manoa.hawaii.edu/privacy/.
