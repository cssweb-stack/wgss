Graduate Applications & Admissions

The WGSS Graduate Certificate application deadlines are March 31 for fall admission and October 31 for spring admission. Applications received after these dates will be considered on a case-by-case basis as space allows.

How to Apply

To apply, complete the online graduate application and submit the supplemental documents described below.

Complete the Online Graduate Application

Prospective WGSS Graduate Certificate students, including those earning the certificate concurrently with another graduate degree, must complete the online application as provided by Graduate Division. All questions concerning the application and associated requirements should be directed to Graduate Division. There is an application fee. GRE results are not required.

Submit Supplemental Documents

All application materials, including supplementary materials and reference letters, should be sent online through the Graduate Division online application.

  • Letter of interest indicating the applicant’s interest in the certificate and long-term personal and professional goals.
  • Letters of recommendation: Either one letter from a WGSS faculty member or three letters that can be released from the applicant’s home graduate program.
  • Writing sample (optional), such as an article or term paper.
  • TOEFL scores: For students from non-English-speaking countries, the minimum scores are 600/100 (paper/Internet).

Post-Baccalaureate Unclassified Students

Post-Baccalaureate Unclassified (PBU) students hold a bachelor’s degree and enroll at UH Mānoa without seeking a specific degree. See Graduate Division’s PBU eligibility and application instructions.

After Admission

Upon admission to the program, students must meet with an interim advisor of their choice to review the Check-Ins and IDP form. The signed form must then be submitted to the department's graduate advisor and secretary.
