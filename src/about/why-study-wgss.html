Why Study Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies?

Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies explores how gender and sexuality intersect with race, culture, and power. Students develop critical thinking, research, and communication skills through interdisciplinary feminist scholarship, with opportunities to connect their studies to their academic interests, professional goals, and communities.

Undergraduate Study

Undergraduate students develop a foundation in interdisciplinary feminist and gender scholarship while strengthening their critical thinking, research, writing, and communication skills. Courses connect these ideas to questions of health, identity, inequality, and community life, complementing study in fields such as psychology. In the department’s student video, majors describe thought-provoking courses and supportive instructors who encouraged them to consider perspectives beyond their own, connect learning with advocacy and community change, and explore career goals such as working with LGBTQ and immigrant youth.

Graduate Study

The WGSS Graduate Certificate offers interdisciplinary feminist scholarship that supports students’ academic and professional goals. Students may pursue the graduate certificate while simultaneously working toward a master's or doctoral degree in another field, or as a standalone certificate program.
