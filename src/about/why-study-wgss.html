Why Study Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies?

Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies explores how gender and sexuality intersect with race, culture, and power. Students develop critical thinking, research, and communication skills through interdisciplinary feminist scholarship, with opportunities to connect their studies to their academic interests, professional goals, and communities.

Undergraduate Study

Our undergraduate program offers a major (BA) and WGSS and Queer Studies certificates.

Graduate Study

Our program offers a Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies (WGSS) Graduate Certificate.

The WGSS Graduate Certificate provides a rigorous, integrated, interdisciplinary, and important educational experience for postgraduate students whose education and career objectives will be enhanced by a creative, critical, and scholarly feminist lens of analysis. Students may pursue the graduate certificate while simultaneously working towards a master's or doctoral degree in another field, or as a standalone certificate program.

Applying for Graduate Study

The WGSS Graduate Certificate application deadlines are March 31 for fall admission and October 31 for spring admission. Applications received after these dates will be considered on a case-by-case basis as space allows.

1) Complete Online Graduate Application

Prospective WGSS Graduate Certificate students, including those earning the certificate concurrently with another graduate degree, must complete the online application as provided by Graduate Division. All questions concerning the application and associated requirements should be directed to Graduate Division. There is an application fee. GRE exam results are not required.

2) Submit Supplemental Documents

All application materials, including supplementary materials and reference letters, should be sent online through the Graduate Division online application.

  • Letter of interest indicating 1) why you are interested in the certificate, and 2) your long-term goals, both personal and professional.
  • Letter(s) of recommendation: Either one letter from a WS faculty member or three letters that can be released from your home graduate program.
  • Writing sample (optional), such as an article or term paper.
  • TOEFL scores: For students from non-English-speaking countries, the minimum scores are 600/100 (paper/Internet).
