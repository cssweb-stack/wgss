Advising & Support

Undergraduate advising helps students explore WGSS programs, choose courses, and plan their studies around their academic and professional goals. The undergraduate advisor can also help students define an area of interest, develop proposals, explore the fully online option, and connect with a faculty advisor suited to their interests.
