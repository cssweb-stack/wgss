Double Majors

Passionate about women's rights and political issues? Want to be formally credentialed in both worlds so you can have an edge in the job market? Then consider double majoring in Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies and another degree of your choice! With careful academic planning, students may be able to complete two majors on time at no extra cost.
