Double Majors

Passionate about women's rights and political issues? Want to be formally credentialed in both worlds so you can have an edge in the job market? Then consider double majoring in Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies and another degree of your choice! Start your planning early so you can graduate on time with two majors at no extra cost.
