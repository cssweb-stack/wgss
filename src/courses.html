Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies (WGSS) Courses

The Department of Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies offers an interdisciplinary curriculum examining gender, sex, and sexuality across global, historical, and intersectional perspectives. Coursework spans lower-division foundations through graduate seminars, engaging critical frameworks in feminist, queer, and Indigenous theories with particular attention to Hawaiʻi, Oceania, and Asia.

{% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 600, 600, '', sidebarClass="sidebar-left-sm") %}

Lower Division (100–200 Level)

Introductory courses establish core concepts in feminist interdisciplinary analysis, LGBTQ studies, and the global history of gender, sex, and sexuality from prehistory to the present. Students explore how gender is constructed and expressed across cultures through topics including psychology, philosophy, world literature, appearance, and ancient religious traditions.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 600, 600, '', sidebarClass="sidebar-left-sm") %}

Upper Division (300–400 Level)

Upper-division coursework applies intersectional feminist and queer frameworks to specialized topics in health, law, politics, labor, media, performance, and environmental philosophy. Students examine global and regional dynamics across Oceania, Asia, and the United States, investigating themes such as Indigenous feminisms, island feminisms, trans* studies, gender violence, and social movements while developing skills in feminist research methods, community internships, and teaching.

{% endcall %} {% call imageTextRow('/media/shared/placeholders/temp.jpg', 600, 600, '', sidebarClass="sidebar-left-sm") %}

Graduate Division (600–700 Level)

Graduate seminars provide advanced training in transnational feminist theory, queer theory, and interdisciplinary research methodologies. Coursework examines critical debates in social policy, criminology, international law, performance, and the African diaspora, culminating in academic publishing preparation and capstone research projects.

{% endcall %}
