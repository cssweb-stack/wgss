Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies at UH Mānoa

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. The department offers an interdisciplinary approach to studying women and gender issues. It emphasizes multicultural and global education and innovative feminist research on gender, sexuality, ethnic and national diversity, and social justice, focusing on women in Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific. The department offers a major (BA) with a fully online option, undergraduate WGSS and Queer Studies certificates, and a graduate certificate.
