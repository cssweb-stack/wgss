Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies at UH Mānoa

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. The department brings an interdisciplinary feminist perspective to the study of gender, sexuality, diversity, and social justice, with a focus on women in Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific. Through multicultural and global education and research, students explore how these issues intersect with race and class to shape everyday experiences. They develop tools to question assumptions, understand different perspectives, and contribute to social change.
