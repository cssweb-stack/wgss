Women, Gender and Sexuality Studies at the University of Hawaiʻi

Our department is proud to offer an interdisciplinary approach to studying women and gender issues. We emphasize multicultural and global education and innovative feminist research on gender, sexuality, ethnic and national diversity, and social justice, focusing on women in Hawaiʻi, Asia, and the Pacific. We offer a major (BA) with a fully online option , undergraduate WGSS and Queer Studies certificates, and a graduate certificate .
